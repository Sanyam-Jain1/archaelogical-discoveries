What to look for in satellite view
  • Likely a theh: a low, rounded, unploughed rise, paler or greyer than the fields, often gullied by rain, with tracks or a shrine on it, and fields that bend around it.
  • Village: roofs, lanes and courtyards. Old villages often sit on a mound, but those are rarely unrecorded. Mark them not a site unless there is bare mound beyond the houses.
  • Brick kiln: an oval trench, a tall chimney and its shadow, stacks of red brick.
  • Dune: sand with ripples, elongated in line with its neighbours.
  • Tank or pond: water or a dry hollow ringed by an embankment.
  • Spoil heap or grove: fresh tipped earth with tracks, or a clump of trees.
